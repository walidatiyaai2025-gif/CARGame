## Why this task is active

`ADS-002` and `PRIV-001` are VERIFIED. `TEST-011` still requires consent/privacy integration and user-data controls; ADS-007 is the first dependency-ready blocker in catalog order and also unlocks PRIV-002.

## Acceptance

- Google UMP is the consent source of truth; no duplicate cached consent-granted preference.
- Consent info refresh runs on launch and required forms are shown before Mobile Ads initialization/requesting.
- `canRequestAds` gates SDK initialization plus banner/rewarded/interstitial request paths.
- Settings exposes a publisher-rendered privacy entry that re-opens Google privacy options when required.
- Runtime eligibility updates after privacy choices without an app restart.
- UMP/Mobile Ads failure never blocks offline/core play.
- First-party analytics stays absent/disabled; ENG-012 remains the owner of future analytics collection.
- PRIV-001 inventory and automated privacy checks reflect the new truth.
- Focused tests, Analyze, full Flutter suite, Debug APK and normal CI pass before verification.

## Non-goals

- No analytics SDK or event collection.
- No production AdMob IDs or credentials.
- No app-side persistence of a duplicate consent decision.
